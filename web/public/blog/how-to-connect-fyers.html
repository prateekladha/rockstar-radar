How to connect your FYERS account to Rockstar Radar

A first-timer's step-by-step guide — about 5 minutes, start to finish.

Updated November 2026 · For new Rockstar Radar users on the NSE (Indian market)

Rockstar Radar is a real-time NSE intraday opening-range breakout radar. It works with your own broker account: you connect FYERS once, log in each morning, and the radar streams live market data to rank intraday setups with entry, stop-loss and target levels. It never sees your password and never fires real orders on its own — you confirm every trade, and paper trading is the default.

What you'll need: a FYERS trading account, and a Rockstar Radar account (sign in with Google — it's free). That's it. No paid API add-on is required.

The 5 steps at a glance

  1. Create a FYERS API app at myapi.fyers.in
  2. Set the redirect URL to https://intraday.live/api/brokers/callback
  3. Paste the App ID & Secret into Rockstar Radar → Broker
  4. Click Connect and log in on the FYERS page
  5. Log in again each trading morning (a SEBI rule)

Step-by-step

  1. Create a FYERS API app

    Go to myapi.fyers.in and sign in with your FYERS credentials. Open Create App, give it any name (e.g. Rockstar Radar), and choose app type Web. FYERS then issues two values you'll use in a moment: an App ID (also called Client ID) and a Secret ID.

  2. Set the redirect URL

    In the same FYERS app, set the Redirect URL to the exact value Rockstar Radar shows on its Broker page:

    https://intraday.live/api/brokers/callback

    This is where FYERS sends you back after you approve access, so the radar can capture your daily token automatically. It must match exactly — same https, no trailing slash. (Already have a FYERS app with its own redirect URL? You can keep it and use the manual paste-the-link option in Rockstar Radar instead.)

  3. Paste your App ID & Secret into Rockstar Radar

    In Rockstar Radar, open the profile menu (top right) → Broker. Choose FYERS as the platform, then paste your App ID and Secret ID into the form. Your secret is encrypted before it's stored — it's never kept in plain text and never shown back to you.

  4. Connect and log in to FYERS

    Click Connect. You're taken to the official FYERS login page, where you sign in and approve access. FYERS then redirects you back to Rockstar Radar, which captures the token and starts streaming live data. You'll see your broker status flip to connected.

  5. Log in again each trading morning

    SEBI requires a fresh broker login every trading day for all Indian brokers, and tokens can't be auto-renewed. So each morning you tap Connect once — ideally before 9:15 IST — to start the day's scan. It's one tap on FYERS' own page; you never re-enter the App ID or Secret.

Your security: you log in on FYERS' own page (OAuth). Rockstar Radar never sees your FYERS password; it only holds an encrypted, single-day access token. Your login, your data, your trades.

Troubleshooting

What you seeFix
"Redirect URL mismatch" / login bounces back with an error The Redirect URL in the FYERS app doesn't exactly match https://intraday.live/api/brokers/callback. Check for a missing https, a trailing slash, or a typo, save it in the FYERS app, and connect again.
"The broker rejected the login. Check your Secret." Re-copy the Secret ID from myapi.fyers.in (no stray spaces), paste it again, and retry. The App ID and Secret must be from the same FYERS app.
Feed says "warming up" or shows no data Make sure you logged in today (the token is daily) and that it's a trading day during market hours (09:15–15:30 IST). Tap Connect again if your morning login has expired.
You pasted the return link but nothing connected If your FYERS app uses its own custom redirect URL, use the manual option: open the login link, sign in, then copy the full URL from your browser's address bar and paste it back into Rockstar Radar.

Frequently asked questions

Does Rockstar Radar see my FYERS password?

No. You log in on FYERS' own page via OAuth. Rockstar Radar never sees or stores your password — only an encrypted daily access token.

Why do I have to log in to FYERS every day?

SEBI rules require a fresh broker login each trading day for all Indian brokers, and programmatic token renewal isn't permitted. So you tap Connect once each morning; it takes a few seconds.

Is a paid FYERS API plan required?

No. Creating an API app at myapi.fyers.in and using it for market data and your own orders is included with a FYERS account at no extra charge.

Will Rockstar Radar place trades on its own?

No. It's semi-automatic: it prepares ranked setups with entry, stop-loss and target, and you confirm every order. Paper trading is the default, and it never fires real orders autonomously.

Which other brokers are supported?

FYERS is fully supported today. Zerodha Kite, Upstox, Angel One, Dhan, Groww, Paytm Money and 5paisa are planned and appear as "coming soon" in the Broker page.
